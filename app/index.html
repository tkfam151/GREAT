HOW TO PLAY

W A S DMove MouseLook around SpaceJump, swim up, or fly up ShiftSprint (fly down while flying) Left clickBreak a block (hold it in Survival) Right clickPlace the block in your hand Middle clickPick the block you're looking at EOpen inventory and crafting 1–9 / WheelChoose hotbar slot FToggle flying (Creative only) TSkip ahead in time F3Debug info EscPause, change mode, or quit to the launcher

Survival

You start with nothing. Hold left click to dig: trees give logs, stone gives cobblestone, grass gives dirt. Open your inventory with E to craft planks, smooth stone, stone bricks, glass and bricks. Watch your hearts: long falls hurt and you can run out of air underwater. Health slowly comes back when you avoid damage.

Creative

Every block is unlimited and breaks instantly. Press F to fly. Grab any block from the creative inventory with E.

You can switch a world between modes at any time from the pause menu.
