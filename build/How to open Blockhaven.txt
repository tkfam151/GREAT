HOW TO OPEN BLOCKHAVEN ON YOUR MAC
==================================

1. Drag Blockhaven into the Applications folder (in this window).

2. Open the Terminal app (Applications > Utilities > Terminal),
   paste this whole line, and press Return:

pkill -9 -f "Blockhaven"; mkdir -p ~/Applications; rm -rf ~/Applications/Blockhaven.app; ditto --noextattr --noqtn /Applications/Blockhaven.app ~/Applications/Blockhaven.app && codesign --force --deep --sign - ~/Applications/Blockhaven.app && open ~/Applications/Blockhaven.app

   It copies Blockhaven into your personal Applications folder
   (~/Applications) without the download quarantine, re-signs that
   copy for your Mac, and opens it.

3. From now on, open Blockhaven from ~/Applications
   (Finder > your home folder > Applications).

Run the same line again whenever you download a new version.

Why? Blockhaven isn't notarized with a paid Apple Developer ID,
so macOS blocks the downloaded copy. The command only affects
Blockhaven; it doesn't change any other security settings.

Shorter alternative that works on many Macs:

    xattr -cr /Applications/Blockhaven.app
