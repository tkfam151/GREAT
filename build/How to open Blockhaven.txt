HOW TO OPEN BLOCKHAVEN ON YOUR MAC
==================================

1. Drag Blockhaven into the Applications folder (in this window).

2. Open the Terminal app (Applications > Utilities > Terminal),
   paste this line, and press Return:

       xattr -cr /Applications/Blockhaven.app

3. Open Blockhaven from Applications or Launchpad as usual.

You only need to do this once per download.

Why? Blockhaven isn't notarized with a paid Apple Developer ID,
so macOS marks the downloaded copy as "quarantined". The command
above removes that mark from Blockhaven only. It doesn't change
any other security settings on your Mac.

Alternative without Terminal: try to open Blockhaven once, then go
to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
"Open Anyway" next to the message about Blockhaven.
